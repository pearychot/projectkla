Why Bangkok

Thailand has no shortage of ambitious students, but few structured chances to build something of their own. In 2024, Pear Chotbunwong saw the appetite firsthand while running workshops for 500+ students across 10 Bangkok schools — the 2026 program brings that work back at scale.

PEAR'S STORY · {{ pearLabel }}

From Stanford back to Bangkok

After my first year at Stanford, I returned to Bangkok with a question: what if more students in Thailand had access to the entrepreneurship education I was receiving?
500+ students · 10 schools

Back home, I taught the entrepreneurship class I'd just taken to 500+ students at 10 Bangkok schools and universities. I named it "Dream Day."
"You gave me the comfort that someone believes in my dreams."
— DREAM DAY PARTICIPANT, BANGKOK UNIVERSITY, SEP 2024
"The Dream Day Workshop was the best day of my entire year."
— DREAM DAY PARTICIPANT, TRIAMUDOM SUKSA SCHOOL, SEP 2024

This year, my Stanford professor is flying to Bangkok to teach the class himself.

If you'd like to work with us, I'd love to hear from you!
